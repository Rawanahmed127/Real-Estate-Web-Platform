# Real Estate Web Platform

A real estate marketplace designed to simplify buying, selling, and renting properties by connecting property owners, real estate agents, buyers, and tenants through a single platform.

# Project Overview

The platform enables users to explore available properties, search using advanced filters, compare listings, save favorite properties, and contact advertisers directly.

Property owners and real estate agents can create accounts, publish property advertisements, upload images, and manage their listings through a dedicated dashboard. An administrative dashboard supports platform management and moderation.

# Key Features

. Property Listings: Browse properties available for sale and rent.
. Advanced Search & Filtering: Search by location, price, property type, and area.
. Property Details: View images, prices, locations, and property specifications.
. Property Comparison: Compare properties to make informed decisions.
. Favorites: Save properties for future reference.
. Recently Viewed: Revisit previously explored properties.
. Direct Contact: Connect with property owners and real estate agents.
. User Accounts: Support property seekers, property owners, and real estate agents.
. Listing Management: Create, edit, publish, and manage property advertisements.
. Image Uploads: Add images to property listings.
. Admin Dashboard: Manage users, listings, and reported advertisements.
. Featured Listings: Promote selected properties.
. Mortgage & Installment Calculator: Estimate potential payments.
